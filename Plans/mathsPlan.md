# 12-Month Intensive Mathematics Plan for Quantitative Finance

**Target:** Master all mathematical concepts required for quantitative finance careers

**Daily Schedule:** 8-10 hours/day, 6 days/week
**Assessment:** Weekly problem sets, monthly comprehensive exams
